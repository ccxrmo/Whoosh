About Whoosh

Whoosh is a social media platform inspired by Twitter/X and the now-defunct Flirb. It's designed to be compatible with almost every device.

Features

Whoosh offers a range of features designed to enhance your social media experience:

  • Customizable profiles!
  • Third-party integrations that grants you the ability to share everything!
  • Real-time updates and notifications
